The Absolute Basics of Tawhid

Tawhid and its categories:

Shaykh Salih Al-Uthaymeen writes:

“Before we begin talking about this great message, we would like to point out that all of the messengers’ messages, from the first of them Noah (Nuh)عليه السلام, to the last of them, Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم all call for monotheism (Tawhid).

Allah said: {And We did not send before you any messenger but to him was it revealed that there is no god but Me, so worship Me} [Al-Anbiya’: 25].

And Allah said: {And We have certainly sent into every nation a messenger, saying, “Worship God and avoid the Taghut”} [An-Nahl: 36].

This is because creation was created for one , which is Allah. They were created to worship Him, so that their hearts would be attached to Him. Deification, glorification, fear, hope, trust, desire, and awe, until they are detached from everything in this world that does not help them in uniting God Almighty in these matters. Because you are a created being, you must belong to your Creator, heart and soul in everything.

That is why the Messengers عليهم السلام, called for this great and important matter: to worship Allah alone, without partners.

The Messengers whom Allah sent to mankind did not call for the unification of worship (Tawhid Al-Uloohiyyah) as they called for the unification of Lordship (Tawhid Al-Rububiyah), because those who deny Tawhid Al-Rububiyah are very few, and even those who deny it in the depths of their souls cannot deny it, unless they have robbed the perceptive minds of the slightest understanding, for they may deny this out of arrogance.

The scholars, may God have mercy on them, divided monotheism into three categories:

One of them: Tawhid Al-Rububiyyah

It is “singling out God Almighty in three matters: creation, dominion, and management.”

The evidence for this is the Almighty’s saying: {Indeed, to Him belongs the creation and the command} [Al-A’raf, 54]. Then consider the opening of this verse with (except), indicating warning and emphasis: {Indeed, to Him belongs the creation and the command} [Al-A’raf: 4], and no other than Him, for this is the creation, and the command is the management.

As for kingship, its evidence is like the Almighty’s saying: {And to God belongs the dominion of the heavens and the earth}[Al-Jathiya: 27], for this indicates that He, Glory be to Him, is alone in kingship, and the evidence is derived from this verse, as previously presented, what is justified by the delay.

Therefore, God Almighty is alone in creation, dominion, and management.

If you say: How do you combine what you have decided with proving creation to other than God, such as the Almighty’s saying: {So blessed be God, the best of creators} [Al-Mu’minun: 14], and such as the prophet صلى الله عليه وسلم regarding the photographers: “It will be said to them: ‘Bring to life what you have created’,” and an example of the Almighty’s saying in the holy hadith: “And who is more unjust than he who creates like my creation?”, then how do you combine your saying: God Alone in creation, and between these texts?!

The answer is to say: Creation is beginning something, and this is specific to God Almighty. As for transforming something from one form to another, it is not a real creation, even if it is called creation in view of formation, but in reality it is not a complete creation. For example: This carpenter made a door from wood, so it is said: He created a door, but the material of this craft that he created is God Almighty. All people, no matter how great their ability, will never be able to create an arak stick, nor to create an atom, nor to create flies.

And listen to the words of Allah: {O humanity! A lesson is set forth, so listen to it ˹carefully˺: those ˹idols˺ you invoke besides Allah can never create ˹so much as˺ a fly, even if they ˹all˺ were to come together for that. And if a fly were to snatch anything away from them, they cannot ˹even˺ retrieve it from the fly. How powerless are those who invoke and those invoked!} [Al-Hajj: 73].

{{Those}: a relative noun that includes everything that is called upon other than God, such as trees, stones, human beings, angels, and other things. All those whom they call upon other than God. {can never create ˹so much as˺ a fly} If each one were to do this alone, his inability would be more likely, {And if a fly were to snatch anything away from them, they cannot ˹even˺ retrieve it from the fly.} Even those who call on other than God, if a fly robbed them of something, they would not be able to rescue it from this weak fly. And if a fly fell on the most powerful king on earth and sucked out some of his perfume, this king would not be able to extract the perfume from this fly. Likewise, if it fell on His food, then God Almighty is the Creator alone.

[Second Section: Monotheism of Divinity]

It is singling out God Almighty for worship. That you should not be a slave to anyone other than God, that you should not worship a king, a prophet, a guardian, an old man, a mother, or a father. You should not worship anyone but God alone, so God Almighty alone is singled out for deification and worship. This is why it is called: monotheism of divinity, and it is called: monotheism of worship. Considering that adding him to God is monotheism of divinity, and considering adding him to the worshiper is monotheism of worship.

Worship is based on two great things, namely love and glorification, which result from them: {Indeed, they used to hasten to do good deeds and call upon Us with hope and dread} [Al-Anbiya’: 90], so with love comes desire, and with glorification comes awe and fear.

That is why worship was commands and prohibitions: commands based on the desire and request to reach the Commander, and prohibitions based on glorification and awe of this great One.

If you love God Almighty, you desire what He has and desire to reach Him, and you seek the path that leads to Him, and you obey Him in the most perfect way, and if His greatness you fear Him, whenever you intend to commit disobedience, you sense the greatness of the Creator, the Almighty, and you flee, {And indeed I intended for it and they intended for it, had he not seen the proof of his Lord. Thus, We will surely turn away from him evil and immorality} [Joseph: 24]. This is from God’s blessing upon you. If you are about to commit a sin, you find God in front of you, so you stand, fear, and distance yourself from the sin, because you worship God with desire and fear.

What is the meaning of worship?

Worship: refers to two things, the action and the object.

It is used to refer to the action, which is worship. It is said: A man worshiped his Lord as an act of worship and devoutness, and calling it worship is in the sense of giving the noun of the infinitive, and we know it by considering the application of it to the action as: “submissiveness to God Almighty out of love and reverence, by doing His commands and avoiding His prohibitions.” And everyone who submits to God is exalted in God, {and glory belongs to God and to His Messenger} [Al-Munafiqun: 8]. It is given to the object, meaning: the one who is worshiped, and in this sense it is known as what Sheikh al-Islam Ibn Taymiyyah defined it with, when he said, may God have mercy on him: “Worship is a comprehensive name for everything that God loves and is pleased with, from words and deeds, both apparent and hidden”.

This thing with which we worship God must be directed to God, and must not be diverted to anything else, such as prayer, fasting, zakat, Hajj, supplication, vows, fear, and trust... and other forms of worship.”

In summary:

The Three Categories of Tawheed

  1. Tawheed Ar Rurubiyyah
    Singling Allah out in His actions (like creation)
  1. Tawheed Al Uluhiyyah
    Singling out Allah in our actions (like ibadah)
  1. Tawheed Al Asma Wa As Sifaat
    Affirming the Names and Attributes as described in Qur’an and Sunnah to our knowledge without denial (Tatil) or likening to creation (Tashbih) with potential suspension of determination
