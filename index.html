About the project

A call to return to the Sufism of the righteous Imam's

السلام عليكم ورحمة الله وبركاته

The aim of this project is to educate people on the reality of Sufism: that it is indeed a part of the dīn, an Islamic science derived from the Qurʾān, Sunnah, and the Righteous Salaf. We share evidence from classical scholars, including those of the Salafī and Atharī traditions.

This project is still growing. We are not scholars, but duʿāt clarifying what taṣawwuf truly is. We welcome sincere correction and invite readers to explore our articles on belief, spiritual refinement, and the wisdom of the scholars.
